04 · The APT circle context

Why the APT circle question is unlike any other right now.

Vi's turnaround is at an inflection point, and APT is a circle where several transitions are landing at once — new leadership presence, 5G expansion, subscriber momentum reversing, and a workforce culture that (as you have written) responds better to structured, patient, localized approaches than to broad national templates.

Q4FY26
Sequential improvement across all seven of the CEO's benchmark parameters — the first "decisive step forward" moment for Vi.
Feb '26
Subscriber addition turned net positive — a milestone that reflects the impact of sustained network investment.
80+
Cities where 5G experience is now live, with 4G coverage expanded to a population of over 48 million.
5
Values the workforce is being asked to reflect: Passion, Boldness, Trust, Speed, Digital.

The reason I mention these is not to demonstrate that I follow the CEO's statements. It is because a turnaround workforce is a different workforce. Retention drivers shift. Manager conversations change. Engagement means something different when the story is starting to turn positive. That is the moment APT is in — and it aligns almost exactly with the localization thesis you have been publicly making.

06 · The honest acknowledgment

Ma'am, I also want to be clear about what I am not.

I do not have the same depth of direct, hands-on HRBP or HR Operations experience as someone who has spent several years working exclusively within the HR function.

I understand that this is an important consideration — particularly for a role that requires practical exposure to payroll operations, statutory and labour compliance, employee lifecycle processes, PD cycles, MIS and SAP-based reporting, and the day-to-day ER work of an HRBP at cluster level. I do not want to overstate my experience or suggest that academic exposure is equivalent to practical HR execution. It is not.

What I can honestly say is that my interest is genuine, my learning agility is one of the things I have most consistently demonstrated across three very different Vi roles in two years, and I am willing to build the practical foundations of the HR function under experienced practitioners — patiently, without shortcuts.
